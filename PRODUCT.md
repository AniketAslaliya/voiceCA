# VoiceCA — The CA's Voice, Automated

## What It Is
**VoiceCA** is a voice-first Chartered Accountant assistant designed specifically for Indian small business owners. Instead of filling forms, users speak their business transactions in natural Hindi, Hinglish, English, or other Indian languages. VoiceCA instantly extracts structured accounting data, calculates taxes, identifies deductions, and provides financial clarity.

## What VoiceCA Understands

### Intent Types (Expanding)
1. **Credit Entry (Udhaari)** — Money you lent to customers
   - Voice: "Patel ko 50,000 rupee ka cloth credit diya"
   - Auto-extracts: Person, amount, date, item
   - Tax: No GST (personal credit)

2. **Expense** — Money you spent
   - Voice: "Office supplies ke liye 5000 rupee kharch kiye"
   - Auto-extracts: Category, amount, date, vendor, GST slab
   - Tax: 18% GST, eligible for 80C deduction
   
3. **Insurance Claim** — Accident or damage claim
   - Voice: "Truck accident mein ₹1,50,000 loss ho gaya"
   - Auto-extracts: Claim type, amount, date, parties involved

4. **Reminders** — Follow-ups needed
   - Voice: "GST return file karne ke liye yaad dilao"
   - Scheduled reminders for compliance

### Tax Intelligence Built In

**GST Calculations:**
- Identifies correct slab (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) per expense type
- Shows taxable amount, GST amount, total amount
- Flags GST-liable vs. GST-exempt transactions

**Deduction Mapping:**
- Section 80C: Office, materials, supplies
- Section 80D: Insurance, professional services
- Section 80TTA: Interest income
- Shows estimated tax savings per deduction

**Category Awareness:**
- Office supplies → 18% GST, office expenses category
- Travel → 5-18% GST depending on mode, travel expense category
- Salaries → 0% GST, direct expense (can't claim as deduction)
- Professional services → 18% GST, can be deducted

## The User Experience

### Workflow
1. **"Kya hua aaj?"** (What happened today?)
   - User taps mic button
   
2. **User speaks naturally**
   - "Aaj Rajesh ko monthly salary 40,000 rupee diya, aur office ke liye 8000 rupee printer ink kharida"
   
3. **VoiceCA instantly shows**
   - Salary entry: ₹40,000, no GST, office expense category
   - Office supply entry: ₹8,000 + ₹1,440 GST (18%), deductible under 80C
   - Next actions: "File invoice for printer ink, claim as office expense"
   
4. **Save and move on**
   - Entries auto-saved to dashboard
   
5. **Financial Summary shows**
   - Total income, total expenses, profit
   - Total GST liable: ₹1,440 (for GST return)
   - Deductible amount: ₹8,000 (saves ~₹2,400 in tax)
