## **Recording Tips**

1. **Screen Recording Tool:**
   - OBS Studio (free, professional)
   - Loom (easiest, built-in editing)
   - ScreenFlow (Mac)

2. **What to Record:**
   - Your screen with the live app
   - Optional: webcam in corner during intro/outro

3. **Pace:**
   - Speak clearly and slowly
   - Pause between sections
   - Let features load naturally

4. **Production Quality:**
   - Good lighting for webcam (if used)
   - Clear microphone audio
   - No background noise
   - Edit out mistakes (Loom has auto-captions)

5. **Upload:**
   - YouTube (unlisted link) or Loom
   - Keep it under 10 minutes
   - Add timestamps in description
