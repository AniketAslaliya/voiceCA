# VoiceCA — Product Brief
## Wednesday 28 May Checkpoint | OpenAI x Outskill Hackathon

---

## The Problem

63 million Indian small businesses run on memory and WhatsApp.
Not because owners are uneducated — but because every existing
software requires typing in English.

A kirana shop owner in Jaipur. An insurance agent in Ahmedabad.
A local contractor in Chennai. They speak fluently. They think clearly.
But the moment a form, a ledger, or a letter appears — they are stuck.

---

## The Solution

**VoiceCA** — Speak your business. AI handles the paperwork.

Speak in Hindi, Hinglish, or English. VoiceCA understands the intent,
extracts the information, and generates the right output — a ledger
entry, a claim log, a follow-up reminder, a formal letter.

No typing. No forms. No English required.

---

## How It Works

1. User taps the mic button and speaks naturally
2. OpenAI Whisper transcribes the audio in real time
3. Codex interprets the intent and extracts structured data
4. VoiceCA shows a confirmation and creates the record
5. Reminders and follow-ups are scheduled automatically

| Use Case | Who uses it | What they say | What happens |
|----------|-------------|---------------|--------------|

| Claim logging | Insurance agent | "Sharma ji car claim 35000" | Claim card + follow-up in 3 days |

---

## Why Codex

Codex is the core intelligence layer — not just a text generator.
It acts as a multi-language intent engine:
reads raw Hindi/Hinglish speech → identifies what the user wants →
extracts entities (names, amounts, dates) → generates structured JSON →
produces confirmation in the user's own language.

This is Codex doing real reasoning work, not surface generation.
