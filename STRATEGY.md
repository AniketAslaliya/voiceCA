# VoiceCA: 4-Hour Sprint Strategy

## Vision
Voice-first Chartered Accountant app for Indian small business owners.
Not claiming best — **proving it through product**.

## Core Competitors & USPs to Match/Exceed
- **Vyapar** — Easy entry, GST handling, multi-user
- **Busy** — Accounting features, tax calculations, inventory
- **TallyPrime** — Professional, complex, steep learning curve
- **ClearTax** — Tax optimization, filing, but complex UI

### Our USP
- **Voice-first** (competitors are form-based)
- **Indian languages** (11+ languages, competitors mostly English)
- **Instant tax clarity** (voice input → tax breakdown in seconds)
- **Beautiful UX** (premium feel, not bureaucratic)

## What We're Building in 4 Hours

### 1. Tax-Aware AI (Backend)
- Upgrade prompt to extract:
  - GST category (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%)
  - Tax impact (taxable amount, tax payable)
  - Deduction eligibility
  - TDS applicability
  - Expense category (Office, Travel, Materials, Salaries, etc.)

### 2. Financial Intelligence Dashboard
- **Summary Card**: Total Income | Total Expense | Profit | Tax Liability
- **Category Breakdown**: Revenue by type, Expenses by category
- **Tax Overview**: GST collected, TDS deducted, Income tax estimate
- **Action Panel**: Urgent items (claims pending, documents missing, etc.)

### 3. Enhanced Entry Cards
- Show **tax impact** alongside entry
- Highlight **next action** (file invoice, claim deduction, etc.)
- Quick edit/reclassify buttons

### 4. UX Polish
- Smooth animations on state changes
- Loading states that feel natural
- Error handling that's helpful, not alarming
- Mobile-first responsive design

## Not Doing (Time Box)
- Full accounting ledger (too complex)
- Invoice generation (nice-to-have)
- Integration with banks (out of scope)
- Multi-user setup (storage/auth needed)

## Success Criteria
1. ✅ Voice input → Structured entry with tax implications
2. ✅ Dashboard shows financial summary in real-time
3. ✅ User can see tax liability instantly
4. ✅ UX feels smooth and premium (not generic)
5. ✅ Works in multiple Indian languages
